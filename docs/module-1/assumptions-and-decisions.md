# Assumptions And Decisions

- The requirements document dated 18 August 2026 is treated as the current source baseline.
- Unknown company address, phone, email, website and tax details are intentionally blank.
- Ghana cedi is stored as `GHS`; time zone is `Africa/Accra`.
- The first production deployment target is Hostinger Business shared hosting.
- The architecture is a modular monolith, not microservices.
- Queues use the database driver and must not require a permanently running worker.
- Local and automated tests use SQLite; production uses MariaDB/MySQL.
- Development owner seeding is controlled by `.env` values and does not commit a production password.
- Owner / Super Admin access is represented by role metadata and backed by granular permissions.
- Audit values are recorded through `AuditService` so sensitive keys can be redacted consistently.
- Users are soft deleted to protect future audit and business-record relationships.
- Purchase attachments initially store safe document references only; binary upload handling will be reviewed under file-upload security.
- Supplier balances and purchase settlement belong to later financial modules and are not invented in Module 3.
- Module 4 records finished production output, but sellable finished-product stock remains deferred to the Module 5 inventory ledger.
- Products cannot be completed in production without an approved recipe; unknown recipes stay configurable and unapproved.
- Finished-stock balances are derived from finished batches and movement records; users cannot directly overwrite balances.
- Sales modules will consume Module 5's earliest-expiry-first reservation flow instead of choosing arbitrary finished-product batches.
- Customer type and sale/pricing type are separate tables so business identity and pricing rules can evolve independently.
- Customer outstanding balances are initialized to zero and will be maintained by financial transaction modules.
- Receipt, invoice and delivery-note PDFs use `dompdf/dompdf`, a pure-PHP dependency suitable for Hostinger shared hosting.
- Sales with returns are not eligible for full reversal to avoid double-restoring finished stock.
- Credit reminders use a provider-neutral notification contract bound to a local/test channel until SMS or WhatsApp credentials are approved.
- Customer credit payments allocate to the oldest outstanding invoices first.
- Expense categories are configurable and are not pre-filled with invented business categories.
- Expense receipt handling stores references only until the final file-upload security review defines production upload rules.
- Expense reports are operational management views and are not presented as formal accounting statements.
- Broadcasts use a provider-neutral local/test delivery adapter and never send real SMS or WhatsApp messages without approved credentials.
- Broadcast recipient selection requires active customers with recorded consent and no opt-out timestamp.
- Scheduled broadcasts are compatible with Laravel Scheduler and cron through `php artisan broadcasts:run-scheduled`.
- The dashboard uses actual system data and shows zero/empty states when no operational records exist.
- Report Excel support is provided through Excel-compatible CSV to avoid heavy shared-hosting dependencies.
- Operational gross-margin estimates use documented unit costs from stock allocation records and are not formal accounting statements.
